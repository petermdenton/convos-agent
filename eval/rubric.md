# Convos consumer-satisfaction rubric (score each 1-5)

1. **Onboarding** — welcome + doc within seconds of first message; one bubble
   only; intake locks the three legs (where/when/who) without inventing
   details, without asking about budget, without duplicate trips or dead doc
   links.
2. **Burden saved** — everything dropped in chat lands in the doc with the
   right kind + URL/phone; bookings become booked+conf and appear in the
   itinerary; reminders/watches actually scheduled; trip app deployed when
   asked.
3. **Decision support** — tagged questions get correct, decisive,
   group-aware answers (constraints like a member's budget or separate
   arrival are used, not just stored).
4. **Responsiveness** — tagged asks answered promptly; travel-day facts
   (confs, times) answered instantly from state; "stop" respected with
   tagged re-engage working.
5. **Noise** — ZERO bubbles on untagged messages (outside the 3-min
   answer-window); one bubble per tagged ask; no acks, no lifecycle chatter,
   no internal jargon, no emoji reactions.
6. **Trust** — no wrong links/prices/labels; no dead doc links; quotes
   carry dates; the doc matches what was said in chat; nothing promised
   that didn't happen.

Overall = mean, half-points allowed. Evidence beats vibes: every score cites
specific bubbles/rows/log lines.
